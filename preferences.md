# Food preferences — the family profile

A family of four: two adults and two kids (ages **8 and 12** as of 2026). This
file is the taste profile the weekly meal planner reads before it sources or
picks recipes (see `how-it-works.md` → "Weekly meal planning"). Keep it current —
just tell Claude when something changes ("we're off pork now", "the 8-year-old
loves noodles") and Claude updates this file.

## Household
- Cook for **4** (2 adults + 2 kids). Family-sized portions; a little leftover is welcome.
- Plan **6 dinners per week**.

## Allergies & restrictions
- **None.** No allergies, no hard dietary restrictions.

## Proteins — all welcome
- Chicken & turkey, beef, pork, and fish & seafood are all fair game. Vary them
  across the week rather than repeating one protein.

## Cuisines
- **Favourite: Mediterranean** (Greek, Italian, Levantine).
- Also happily in rotation: **Middle Eastern, Mexican / Latin American,
  Indian / South Asian, East & Southeast Asian.** Open to others too.

## Keep it mild / dislikes
- **No very spicy / hot dishes** — the kids' palates. Warm spices and gentle
  flavour are great; keep chili heat out of the dish and offer it on the side for
  the adults.
- No other strong dislikes reported yet. (Add them here as we learn.)

## Weeknight cooking
- Target **~30 minutes** for most weeknight dinners — quick and simple.
- An occasional more-involved "project" recipe is welcome (weekends).

## Vegetarian scope
- **Lacto-ovo:** eggs, cheese, yogurt, and butter are all fine in the vegetarian
  recipes.

## Kids in the kitchen
For the two "kids can make it" recipes each week:
- **8-year-old:** no sharp knives, no hot stove or oven unsupervised. Their
  recipes are assembly / measuring / mixing / spreading — an adult pre-cuts
  anything that needs a knife. Lean to **no-cook** for this one.
- **12-year-old:** can use the **stovetop and oven with light supervision**.
- So each week: one no-heat/assembly recipe (for the 8yo) and one simple
  stovetop or oven recipe (for the 12yo).

## The weekly six (planner rules)
Each week the planner picks **6 recipes**:
- **2 vegetarian** (lacto-ovo)
- **2 simple meat-and-veg** — a protein plus a vegetable/side
- **2 kid-friendly** — one no-heat for the 8-year-old, one stovetop/oven for the 12-year-old

Constraints: keep dinners **~30 min and mild**; **vary cuisines and proteins**
across the six and from week to week; **don't repeat** a recipe used in the last
~3–4 weeks. Prefer recipes already in `recipes.md`; add one or two **new** ones
when the collection is thin or for variety. Then file every ingredient onto the
grocery list, deduped, each tagged with its recipe (see the capture protocol).
