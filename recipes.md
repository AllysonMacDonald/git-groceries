## Seafood Chowder
*Classic New England style. Serves 4–6. ~45 min.*

### Ingredients
- [ ] white fish (cod or haddock), ~½ lb
- [ ] shrimp, ~½ lb
- [ ] scallops, ~½ lb
- [ ] bacon, 3–4 slices
- [ ] potatoes, 3–4 medium
- [ ] onion, 1
- [ ] celery, 2 stalks
- [ ] carrot, 1
- [ ] garlic, 2–3 cloves
- [ ] butter, 3 tbsp
- [ ] flour, 3 tbsp (for the roux)
- [ ] milk, 2 cups
- [ ] heavy cream, 1 cup
- [ ] corn, 1 cup (optional)
- [ ] fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)
- [ ] bay leaf, 1–2
- [ ] fresh parsley, to finish
- [ ] salt & pepper

### Method
1. **Bacon:** Dice and crisp in the pot over medium heat. Lift out, leave the fat.
2. **Soften veg:** Add butter to the fat. Cook diced onion, celery, and carrot
   ~5 min until soft. Add minced garlic, thyme, and bay leaf; cook 1 min.
3. **Roux:** Stir in flour, cook 1–2 min to coat everything.
4. **Simmer:** Slowly whisk in milk, then add diced potatoes. Simmer gently
   ~15 min until potatoes are fork-tender (don't boil hard or the dairy splits).
5. **Seafood:** Add fish (cut in chunks), shrimp, scallops, and corn. Simmer
   5–7 min just until the seafood is opaque and cooked — it's quick, don't overdo.
6. **Finish:** Stir in the cream, warm through. Season with salt & pepper.
   Pull the bay leaf. Top with the crispy bacon and chopped parsley.

**Notes:** Frozen seafood works — thaw and pat dry first. Serve with the
crackers or bread you've got on hand.

## Classic Three-Bean Salad
*Tangy sweet-sour picnic salad. Serves 6–8. ~15 min + chill. No cooking.*

### Ingredients
- [ ] green beans, 1 can (~14 oz), drained
- [ ] kidney beans, 1 can (~14 oz), drained & rinsed
- [ ] chickpeas, 1 can (~14 oz), drained & rinsed
- [ ] red onion, ½ small, finely diced
- [ ] red bell pepper, 1, diced
- [ ] fresh parsley, small handful, chopped (optional)

**Dressing (sweet-sour vinaigrette):**
- [ ] red wine vinegar, ⅓ cup
- [ ] olive oil, ¼ cup
- [ ] sugar, ⅓ cup
- [ ] salt & black pepper, to taste

### Method
1. **Dressing:** Whisk vinegar, oil, sugar, salt, and pepper until the sugar
   dissolves. Taste — it should be brightly sweet-sour; adjust vinegar/sugar.
2. **Combine:** In a big bowl, toss the three drained beans with the red onion,
   bell pepper, and parsley.
3. **Dress & chill:** Pour the dressing over, toss to coat. Cover and
   refrigerate at least 1 hour (ideally 3–4, or overnight) so the beans soak
   up the tang. Toss again before serving.

**Notes:** Better the next day. Keeps 4–5 days in the fridge. Traditional
version uses canned yellow (wax) beans in place of chickpeas — swap if you
prefer. Drain well before serving so it isn't watery.
